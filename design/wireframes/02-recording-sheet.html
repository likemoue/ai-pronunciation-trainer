2 Recording sheet

On phones the recording popup is a bottom sheet (max height 100dvh); from 768 px it is a centred dialog (page 8) with the same parts.

Anatomy and rules

  • Fixed header: state dot (+ “REC”), title, timer “0:04 / 0:30”, ✕ (44 px). Middle scrolls: “Read aloud” + the sentence in its own scroll area, the waveform (96 px), the hint. Fixed actions at the bottom, above the safe area.
  • The backdrop never closes the sheet (today one tap discarded the take). ✕, Cancel / Close and Escape do.
  • Focus: title on open, Stop while recording, title while checking, the primary action on errors; on close, back to the mic, or to the result title after a score. The page behind is inert (with a focus trap where inert is missing).
  • Announcements: one polite region in the sheet for phase changes; errors use role="alert"; countdowns speak once, not every second.
  • Every error says what happened and what to do, and has its own state below; the recording is kept whenever sending it again could work.
