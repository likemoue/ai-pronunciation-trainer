Phase 4 wireframes: overview

First deliverable of phase 4 (frontend: UX, mobile first, accessibility, in-app browsers). Approved by the user on 2026-11-16 with the decisions below; the screens are built from these wireframes and context/design-system.md.

How to read the wireframes

~ Almost✗ Needs workno mark = Correctabc wrong letters (bold, dotted underline)Primary Secondary Disabledbox labelled “Phase 4 (decided, Q3)” = the typical-mistake hint

Grey only: if a state reads here, it reads without colour. Every frame is at true CSS size (phones 360×640, the in-app viewport 360×560 under the host app’s bar, 320×568, desktop 1280×800). Each caption names the state; the notes under it give the behaviour, focus and announcements, and what changes against today (Changed, Fixed, New). The pages have no JavaScript and load nothing from other sites.

Screens

  1. 1 Main screen, mobile: 13 frames: loading, ready, failed, voice playing, after a result, a 60-word text, offline, cannot record, 320 px, Vietnamese.
  2. 2 Recording sheet: 22 frames: preparing to every microphone error, kept recordings, in-app guidance, long text (360 and 320 px), Vietnamese.
  3. 3 Result card: 8 frames: excellent, good, needs work, nothing recognised, a long sentence, the typical-mistake hint (phase 4, Q3) in EN and VI, and the ✓-on-every-word option (not chosen, Q2).
  4. 4 In-app browser banner: 10 frames: Zalo, Facebook and Messenger on Android and iPhone, link copied, copy blocked, dismissed, in the sheet.
  5. 5 Onboarding + mic check: 10 frames: the 3-step tour (EN, VI), the 3-second microphone check and where to reopen it.
  6. 6 Progress sheet: 7 frames: empty, with progress, overflow, the “New learner” confirmation, after clearing, one word, Vietnamese.
  7. 7 “My text” sheet: 10 frames: empty, ready, keyboard open, the 30 s warning, three errors, loading, back on the page, Vietnamese.
  8. 8 Desktop (1280 × 800): 3 frames: before a result, after a result with its buttons, the recording dialog.

Decisions (approved by the user, 2026-11-16)

  1. Q1. Score thresholds: the page follows the server: titles 90/70/60; ring, chime and words 70/60; no scoring change.
  2. Q2. Marks in the sentence: only on ~ and ✗ words; ✓ stays in the word detail, the legend and each word’s screen-reader name.
  3. Q3. Typical-mistake hint: in phase 4; the API gets a per-word mistake-kind field; scores and labels unchanged.
  4. Q4. Correct colour: teal-green #0f766e, with red #b91c1c for Needs work and wrong letters.

Already decided (reflected, not asked again)

  • English stays the default; an EN | VI toggle in the header, remembered on the device.
  • No privacy notice and no policy page.
  • Keep today’s look and codify it; fix AA contrast; add a colour-blind-safe status palette.
  • Nothing from a CDN or a third party; no inline script or style; recordings never stored; no accounts.

Assumptions (say if one is wrong)

  • The look stays today’s (Inter, indigo, rounded cards, soft shadows); the tokens are codified from static/css/style.css with the AA fixes in context/design-system.md. No design generator was used.
  • Default level Easy; chip order Easy, Medium, Hard, Random. EN by default, the choice remembered in localStorage.
  • Breakpoints: below 768 px the bar and bottom sheets; from 768 px inline controls and centred dialogs; from 1024 px two columns.
  • Score meaning line: “The computer heard N% of the sentence as written” (the score is the share of letters heard as written).
  • “Heard” replaces “Yours” in the word detail (phase 3 plan: honest labels).
  • “Today”, the streak and dates follow Vietnam time (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh).
  • Offline: the mic stays usable (the take is kept and can be sent later from the sheet); Next is disabled.
  • Reading-time estimate at 110 words a minute; the 30 s warning from about 26 s.
  • A word leaves “Words to review” after two Correct readings; at most 12 shown at once.
  • “New learner” clears progress, words to review, the draft and the tour flag, and keeps the language.
  • The in-app banner is dismissed for the visit (sessionStorage) and comes back on the next visit.
  • In-app menus (••• → “Open in browser” / “Open in Safari”) and the intent link work as drawn; to be checked on Zalo, Facebook and Messenger, Android and iPhone, before release.
  • Vietnamese needs Inter’s Vietnamese subset (about 10 KB, served by the app like the other fonts).
  • “Saved in this browser only” (progress sheet) is a functional note, not a privacy notice.
