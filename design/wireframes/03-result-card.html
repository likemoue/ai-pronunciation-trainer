3 Result card

The result card appears directly under the sentence card. Frames here are 360 px wide and not clipped. The dashed line is the fold: the top edge of the bottom bar on a 360 × 640 screen.

Anatomy (top to bottom)

  1. Score ring + title + one plain line saying what the number means (no “native-like” claim).
  2. “Words to practise” chips (✗ first, at most 4) and the detail of the selected word: Correct IPA + Listen | Heard IPA + Replay.
  3. My recording (replay of the whole take), what the computer heard, and a one-line legend.
  4. Desktop only: Try again and Next sentence buttons (page 8). On phones the bar holds them.

In the sentence, wrong letters are bold with a dotted underline (and the Needs-work colour); each Almost or Needs-work word ends with a boxed mark ~ or ✗. Correct words carry no mark (decided, Q2).

One tier table for ring, title, chime and word labels
