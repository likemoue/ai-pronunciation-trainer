1 Main screen, mobile

One column, mobile first, at 360 × 640 CSS px. Below 768 px the controls live in a fixed bottom bar; from 768 px they are inline under the sentence (page 8).

Key layout decisions

  • Order: header (brand, EN | VI, ★ points) → level chips → sentence card → result card → “How it works” → footer. Only the bottom bar is fixed.
  • Bar: Listen · Mic · Next, the mic largest (64 px), labels under each button and adapting to the state (Stop, Try again, Can’t record). DOM order puts the bar right after the sentence card, so keyboard and screen-reader order is sentence → controls → result.
  • Sentence card: “Read this aloud”, the sentence (22 px; 18 px above 20 words), IPA, Slow; “My text” at its top right.
  • Messages live where they apply: skeleton and errors in the card; offline and “can’t record” as notices docked above the bar; recording messages in the sheet. No toasts, no status line.
  • When the screen is shorter than 480 px (a phone held sideways), the bar stops being fixed and sits under the sentence, so it never covers half the screen.
